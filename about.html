About Tema Coast Clearing

We are a licensed customs clearing and forwarding company working out of Tema Port, handling import documentation for businesses across Ghana.

Why we exist

Clearing cargo at Tema Port should not be a guessing game. Too often importers are told a container "is being processed" for weeks, with no paperwork shown and no clear answer about what is actually holding it up.

We built this company around a simple idea: the importer should always know exactly where their cargo stands, and exactly what we have filed on their behalf. That means a written quote before we start, copies of every declaration, and a WhatsApp thread where you talk to the person handling your file — not a call centre.

We tell clients when a delay is in our hands and when it is not. That honesty costs us some short-term comfort and earns us long-term business.

How we work

Four steps, no mystery.

1

Send your documents

Bill of lading, invoice and packing list by WhatsApp or email. We confirm what we received.

2

Written quote

You receive the duty and our charges in writing before anything is filed.

3

We clear

Declaration, assessment, examination and terminal release — handled at the port.

4

Collection or delivery

Collect from the terminal, or we arrange haulage to your destination.

Questions about a shipment?

Tell us what is arriving and we will tell you what it will cost to clear.
