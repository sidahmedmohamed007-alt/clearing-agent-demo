Our services

Import clearance and forwarding at Tema Port — containers, vehicles, transit cargo and general goods.

📦

Container clearing (FCL & LCL)

Complete customs clearance for containerised imports arriving at Tema Port.

  • ✓Customs declaration (Single Window / ICUMS)
  • ✓Duty and tax assessment
  • ✓Physical examination attendance
  • ✓Terminal release and delivery order
🚗

Vehicle & Ro-Ro clearance

Clearing vehicles and rolling equipment arriving by Ro-Ro vessel.

  • ✓Duty computation on vehicle value
  • ✓Age and depreciation assessment
  • ✓Terminal handling and release
🚚

Transit & forwarding

Transit documentation and onward movement to landlocked destinations.

  • ✓Transit declarations to Burkina Faso, Niger and Mali
  • ✓Bond and guarantee coordination
  • ✓Haulage arrangement and tracking
📄

Documentation & advisory

Getting the paperwork right the first time, so clearance is not delayed later.

  • ✓HS code classification guidance
  • ✓Import permit and exemption documentation
  • ✓Pre-arrival documentation review

What you need to send us

Have these ready and we can quote quickly.

  • ✓Bill of lading (or airway bill)
  • ✓Commercial invoice and packing list
  • ✓Import declaration form (IDF) where applicable
  • ✓Tax identification and company details
  • ✓Permits or exemptions if your goods require them
