What we clear

Import and transit clearance for containers, vehicles and general cargo arriving at Tema Port.

📦

Container clearing

Full customs clearance for FCL and LCL container imports — declaration, duty assessment, examination and release.

🚗

Vehicle & Ro-Ro clearance

Clearing of vehicles and rolling stock arriving by Ro-Ro vessel, including duty computation and terminal release.

🚚

Transit & forwarding

Transit documentation and onward forwarding to Burkina Faso, Niger and Mali, plus haulage coordination.
